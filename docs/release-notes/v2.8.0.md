## What's new in 2.8.0

Quieter phone notifications while you are at the PC, a sidebar you can trim and a tidier Settings menu. Your settings move to config schema 20 by themselves: the new options start as before (no delay, every sidebar block shown).

### Notifications

- **Delay phone notices**: **Settings > Notifications > Delay phone notices (seconds)** makes the phone push, Discord and ntfy wait before they go out (for example 120). A notice is dropped if, meanwhile, you answer the agent, open or focus that console, or have the app open on the phone. While you keep using the PC it keeps waiting, and it goes out once the PC has been still for the whole delay. 0 (the default) sends at once as before. Desktop notifications are not affected, and `snaos notify` is never delayed.

### Sidebar

- **Choose what the sidebar shows**: **Settings > Appearance > Sidebar** hides or shows the agents board button, Teams, Shared with me, the sharing and phone indicators and the usage bars. Your workspaces and the Settings button always stay. Hiding the usage bars does not stop reading usage.

### Settings

- **A clearer order**: Models, Claude accounts and AI usage now sit together, followed by the console and repo sections, then notifications, the phone, sharing and connections, then the general preferences, with system and info at the end. Nothing moved between sections, and search finds everything as before.

### Teams (Labs)

- **Blocks sooner on runs without progress**: a task whose runs make no progress now waits 5 and then 10 minutes and is blocked with a card in **Needs you** on the third run in a row (it was the fourth, after a 20 minute wait). A run that makes progress still resets the count.
