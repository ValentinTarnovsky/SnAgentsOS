## What's new in 2.3.0

Answering on one device now clears the alert on the other, agents can look at each other's consoles, a real find and replace in the editor, model and effort from the phone, and two Claude accounts with automatic switching. Your settings move to config schema 16 by themselves (new Claude accounts settings with their defaults, nothing else changes).

### Alerts across devices

- **Answer once, cleared everywhere**: when you answer a console on the phone, the PC stops flashing in the taskbar, its notification goes away and the red badge in the sidebar clears. Answer on the PC and the phone's red badge, the "(1)" in its title and the stale tray notifications clear too.
- A result you already watched finish on one device no longer shows up as unread on the other.

### Desktop

- **Find and replace in the editor**: Notes and Files get a VS Code style find box, readable at any zoom, with match case, whole word and regex, an "N of M" counter and replace one or all (Ctrl+F, Ctrl+H, F3). The terminal search is unchanged (Ctrl+Shift+F).
- **Drawers close on an outside click**: clicking a console beside Notes or Files closes the drawer. Pin it from its header to keep it open.
- **Collapse folders** in Files now always does something: it collapses every folder and clears the filter.

### Claude accounts

- **Two Claude accounts, one click to switch**: save your primary and secondary login in Settings > Claude accounts and switch from there, from the account chip in the sidebar usage, from the phone or with `snaos account use`. Running consoles follow the switch without a restart.
- **Automatic switch (off by default)**: when the primary reaches 99% of its 5 hour or weekly limit, SnAgentsOS moves to the secondary, and goes back once the primary's limit resets. If a switch ever fails, it goes back to the previous account and tells you. Automatic switching between subscriptions may be subject to Anthropic's terms of service; review them before turning it on.
- Logins are stored encrypted on this PC and never leave it.

### Phone

- **Model, effort and Ultracode**: a new sheet (the chip next to the cache timer, or the menu) changes the model, the effort level and Ultracode of a Claude console without typing commands. For Codex it opens the model picker. Commands are typed for you; Enter is only sent if you turn on "Apply with Enter", and only when the console is idle.
- "Chat not available" for a Codex console without a session shows once instead of on every open.

### Agents and CLI

- **Agents can watch other consoles**: `snaos peek <console>` shows what a console is doing (state, what it waits for, last message, last lines), and `snaos chat <console>` returns its conversation, for Claude and Codex.
- **More control from the CLI**: workspace notes (`snaos notes ls|get|new|set|append|rm|open`), consoles (`kill`, `restart --resume|--fresh`, `rename`, `focus`), workspaces (`ws new|rename|close --yes|fav`), `snaos usage` and `snaos account`.
