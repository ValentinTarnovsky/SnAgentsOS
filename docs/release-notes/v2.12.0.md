## What's new in 2.12.0

The voice now works like a narrator: when a console finishes, it tells you what the console answered, not only that it finished.

### Hands-free voice (Labs)

- **It reads the reply**: when a console finishes you hear "Terminó API" and then its reply.
  - A short reply is read as it is. A long one is summarized in a sentence or two by a quick Haiku run on your own Claude login.
  - When several consoles finish together, each one is read in turn, in the order they finished, never two voices at once.
  - Sending a new prompt to a console cuts its reply short, and the next one speaks.
  - Turn **Read the reply** off in **Settings > Voice > Narration** to hear only the name, as in 2.11.0.
- **No microphone needed**: narration now works with hands-free off, as soon as the Voice lab is on. Voice commands ("Agente, ...") still need hands-free. To narrate only while hands-free listens, turn off **Speak with hands-free off**.
- **The console you are looking at too**: a console on screen is narrated as well. To hear only about consoles you do not see, turn off **Also the console you are looking at**. A muted console is never narrated.
- **Stop button**: while the voice speaks, the sidebar shows **Speaking** with a button that cuts what it says, and the next item plays.
- The status chime stays quiet while the voice narrates, with hands-free on or off (**No status chime while the voice narrates**).
- Reading a reply on its own does not mark the console read, so the badges still show what finished while you were away.
- The narrator mod warning in **Settings > Voice** now looks at the mod's own switch: once you run `/narrator off`, it goes away.
- With ElevenLabs in **Everything** mode, every summary counts toward your monthly character cap. Past the cap, the local voice takes over.
