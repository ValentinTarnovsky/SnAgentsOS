## What's new in 2.9.1

Two fixes for Teams runs that got stuck asking you for help they did not need.

### Teams (Labs)

- **Long plans in parts**: Claude Code refuses a Bash command over 10,000 characters, and a team run has nobody to approve it, so a long plan could never be sent. `snaos team plan --part` (and `snaos team hire --part`) now keeps one part of the text, and the call without `--part` sends all the parts joined by a line break. A refused call keeps the parts for the next try. Every Claude run is told to keep its commands under 9,000 characters and to split a long plan this way.
- **QA tasks of its own**: the Lead can give QA a task of its own, such as a full review before a deploy. QA now plans it with `team plan` and closes it with `team done` and its report, by the same rule as a read-only Dev. Before, such a task could neither plan nor close and stayed stuck in **Needs you**.
