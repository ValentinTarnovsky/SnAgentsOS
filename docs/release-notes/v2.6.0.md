## What's new in 2.6.0

Share between PCs now lets you decide what each guest can use, one thing at a time: a guest can edit the workspace notes and browse its files (read only), apart from what they can do in the consoles. Nothing changes in your settings: shares you already have keep working exactly as before until you change them.

### Share between PCs

- **Permissions per guest**: View only, Type only and Full control now decide only what a guest can do in the consoles. Next to them, each workspace share has its own switches: **New and close consoles**, **Chat**, **Changes**, **Notes** (off, read, or read and write) and **Files** (off or read only). Picking a level in the Share dialog fills sensible defaults (Full control turns everything on) and you adjust each one after.
- **Set them for each guest**: the approval prompt lets you give that guest other permissions than the share's, and **Settings > Sharing** edits them live, per share and per connected guest. A guest you limited by hand never gets more when you change the share later.
- **Edit notes from the share**: with Notes set to read and write, a guest creates, edits and deletes the workspace notes. Notes save on their own while typing, and a note that changed on your PC meanwhile shows Reload or Keep mine. A very long note (over 40 KB) stays read only for guests.
- **Browse the workspace files**: with Files on, a guest opens the workspace folder as a tree and reads any file in a read-only editor, with Markdown preview and images. It follows your changes while the drawer is open. A guest can never create, rename, delete or save a file.
- **Secrets stay hidden**: guests never see `.git` nor files like `.env`, private keys and certificates (`*.pem`, `*.key`, `id_rsa`...) or `.npmrc` and `.netrc`, not even through a link. Your own Files drawer still shows them.
- **Older versions keep working**: a guest on SnAgentsOS 2.5 or earlier can still join; for them notes stay read only and there is no Files drawer, and **Settings > Sharing** marks them as an older version.
- Changing a guest's level no longer adds or removes these extras: use the switches for that.

### Desktop

- **`.git` hidden everywhere in Files**: the Files drawer no longer shows `.git` folders of submodules, in any letter case, nor a junction or link that points into one.
