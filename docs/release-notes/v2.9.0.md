## What's new in 2.9.0

Teams can now run on autopilot: once the goal is clear, the team approves, answers and integrates on its own, and **Needs you** keeps only what it cannot settle alone.

### Teams (Labs)

- **Autopilot**: a new **Autopilot** switch in the team header, next to Auto dispatch (off by default; turning it on turns auto dispatch on too). While it is on and the team is not paused, the team:
  - approves the plans, final OKs, permissions and hires that wait for you;
  - answers an agent's question with the option the agent recommends;
  - integrates a clean merge, and sends a merge conflict or a failed verify back to its Dev (up to 3 times in a row per task).
  Each decision is recorded as yours and sends no notification. What it cannot settle stays in **Needs you** and notifies as before: the definition trust, an auth or billing pause, system cards, `team needs`, free text, yes or no and multiple answer questions, a checkout with uncommitted changes and a merge that changes the team definition.
- **Recommended answers**: agents now mark the answer they would pick with `snaos team ask --recommended <n>` (an option ending in "(recommended)" counts too). The option shows a **Recommended** tag on the desktop and on the phone, with or without autopilot.
