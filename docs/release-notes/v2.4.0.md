## What's new in 2.4.0

Share a console or a whole workspace with another SnAgentsOS, dictate into any console from the PC, answer plans and permissions from the phone's Chat view, `claude` and `codex` typed in a PowerShell show up as agents, `/rename` renames the console, up to eight Claude accounts in priority order, and Windows updates that keep your taskbar pin. Your settings move to config schema 17 by themselves: the new settings get their defaults, and the Codex status integration turns on once (you can turn it off again and it stays off).

### Share between PCs

- **Share a console or a workspace**: right-click a console header and choose **Share console...**, or use the share button in the workspace header or the sidebar menu. You get an invite to copy and send to the other PC; the other side pastes it in **Shared with me** in its sidebar and sees your console or workspace live, at your console's size.
- **Over Tailscale or your LAN**: the invite only lists Tailscale and private LAN addresses, and the connection is end-to-end encrypted with keys pinned by the invite. Nothing goes through a server. The dialog shows the share port (4527 by default) and can add the Windows firewall rule for it.
- **Three permissions**: **View only**, **Type only** (type without send: they can type and edit the line and press Esc, but Enter, Ctrl+C, Ctrl+D, multi-line pastes and anything that would run, submit or answer a prompt are blocked, and only Esc gets through while an agent waits on a permission) and **Full control**. Full control on a workspace also lets the guest open consoles from your presets, close consoles, and read Chat, Notes and Changes (read only).
- **Password or approval**: set a password for unattended access, or leave it empty and approve each connection from a prompt that shows the guest's name, address and key fingerprint, with a choice of permission. A guest you already let in can reconnect without a new approval for 15 minutes after it disconnects.
- **You stay in charge**: your keystrokes always win (the guest is paused while you type), the console shows who is connected and who is typing, and **Settings > Sharing** lists every share with its guests: change the permission live, pause, set or clear a password, disconnect a guest or revoke the share. Shares can expire after 1, 8 or 24 hours, or last until you revoke them.
- The guest never sees your other workspaces, usage, accounts or Quick Commands, and links in shared output always ask before opening.

### Desktop

- **Dictation (Windows)**: a microphone button in each console header, **Dictate** in the console menu, `Ctrl+Shift+H` and a mic in the global prompt. Words are typed where the cursor is, on one line and never with Enter, SSH consoles included. For the best quality turn on **Online speech recognition** in Windows privacy settings; when it is off SnAgentsOS falls back to the offline Windows recognizer, says so and offers a button to the setting. While an agent waits on a permission, plan or question, or a numbered menu is on screen, nothing is typed: the text waits until you press **Insert**. Engine, language and the header button are in **Settings > Terminal > Dictation**.
- **Agents typed in a PowerShell**: running `claude` or `codex` (or an alias of them) in a plain PowerShell console now gives it a sidebar row with the agent's icon and a small Claude or Codex badge. The row goes away when you exit back to the prompt; the console stays a shell. You can turn this off in **Settings > Models**.
- **`/rename` renames the console**: `/rename Foo` in Claude or Codex renames the console. A name you gave the console by hand is kept until the agent is renamed again. Toggle it in **Settings > Models**.
- **Codex status is on by default**: the Codex row no longer disappears after a turn when the title changes. Without the Codex CLI installed, Settings shows a neutral "not found" line and the integration starts once Codex is installed.
- The app window now denies web permissions it does not use, camera and microphone included.

### Phone

- **Answer from the Chat view**: plan approvals (including "Yes, and use auto mode") and "Always allow" on permissions now work right from Chat, without opening the terminal. If the menu changed on the PC in the meantime, the phone tells you instead of pressing the wrong option. For Codex requests, elicitations and other prompts without a card, a key row (1, 2, 3, Esc and Terminal) answers in place, never with Enter.
- **Model and effort moved to the menu**: the chip that covered the console title is gone. "Model and effort" is now the first row of the three-dot menu, with the current model and effort next to it.
- **Pick any Claude account**: with more than two accounts, "Change account" opens a list of all of them in priority order.

### Claude accounts

- **Up to 8 accounts in priority order**: reorder them with Up and Down in **Settings > Claude accounts**, and save the current login as a new account. Automatic switching walks the order when an account runs out, and the return steps back up as limits reset, stopping at the account you picked last.
- `snaos account use <n>` picks an account by its position; `snaos account ls` shows a `#n` column.
- Going back to 2.3.0 keeps its accounts file untouched.

### Updates

- **Silent Windows updates that keep the pin**: the app closes, updates and reopens itself, keeping the taskbar pin and shortcuts. Windows may ask for permission, on all-users installs only. If permission is declined or the setup does not finish, the next start says the update did not finish and offers **Retry**.
- The pin is kept from the update after 2.4.0 on. The update from 2.3.0 to 2.4.0 still runs the old updater and may unpin the app one last time; pin it again once and it stays.
- A pinned portable now relaunches the portable `.exe` you started, not its temporary unpacked copy.

### Known limits

- Agent detection in a console works only in PowerShell with shell integration. cmd, WSL and SSH consoles keep the title and hook based status. A quoted path with spaces as the command, or a function that wraps `claude`/`codex`, is not detected.
- `/rename` inside an SSH console is not detected (there is no local signal).
- "Type only" (type without send) knows the key rules of shells, Claude and Codex. For other full-screen programs (custom TUIs, editors) it is advisory: treat it as a safety net, not a sandbox, and share as View only when that matters.
- A host restart drops the 15 minute reconnect window: guests without a password are asked to be approved again. "Pause all sharing" resets on restart.
