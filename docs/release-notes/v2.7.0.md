## What's new in 2.7.0

Teams, a new Labs feature: agents with roles that work on one of your repos without a terminal, from a task board, with your approval for plans and results, a safe **Integrate** into your base branch and the board on the phone. Every console also tells you more: what its agent is doing right now, why its last turn failed and until when, when it has gone quiet, how full its context is and what it spent. Also new: a chat view of agent consoles (Labs), Ctrl+click on `file:line` in the terminal, several schedules per console with cron rules and calmer notifications. Your settings move to config schema 19 by themselves: a console's schedule becomes the first of its schedules and the new settings get their defaults, with both Labs features off.

### Teams (Labs)

- **Off until you turn it on**: Teams lives in **Settings > Labs** and is off by default. While it is off nothing of it loads, runs or shows. Turned on, a **Teams** section appears in the sidebar and `Ctrl+Shift+O` opens it over your workspaces, without closing any console.
- **What a team is**: a set of agents with roles (Lead, Dev, QA and Research by default), each with its provider (Claude Code or Codex), model, effort, access and how many of them, working on one repo (plus up to 4 extra repos) without a terminal. The team's definition is a few Markdown files in the repo, so it can travel with it; the board, the runs and the approvals stay on your PC.
- **Setup and trust**: **New team** proposes a definition you edit (goal, rules, base branch, runs at a time, roles and a verify command, with a suggestion read from the repo) and shows the exact files before writing them; a repo that already has one is adopted after you review it. Agents only ever read the copy you approved. When the files change in the repo, the team waits until you **Review definition**, which lists the risks first (more access, new tools, another provider, more agents, a new verify command, an extra repo, a new routine, changed instructions) and then the diff of every file, with hidden characters shown.
- **The board**: **Board**, **Agents** and **Needs you** tabs. Five columns (To do, In progress, Review, Done, Blocked) with drag and drop, search and an assignee filter, priorities, tasks that wait for other tasks, and comments the agent reads on its next run. What you set always wins: a field you change is pinned, and the Lead cannot overwrite it until you release it.
- **The Lead**: write to the Lead from the Agents tab. It reads the board, splits the team goal into tasks, assigns them and answers you in its own conversation. On an empty board, **Ask the Lead to set up the tasks** starts it right away.
- **Plans and approvals**: a task that needs a plan runs first in plan mode (read only) and waits for you: **Approve**, **Ask for changes** (with a note the agent reads) or **Reject**. The final OK, a role the Lead wants to hire and a permission an agent was denied come to **Needs you** the same way, each new one with a desktop notification. If the team allows it, the Lead decides small plans itself.
- **QA rounds**: a finished task goes to the team's QA agent. Asking for changes sends it back to the Dev with the comment, and the third round blocks the task with a card. Without a QA role the task goes straight to your final OK.
- **A worktree per task**: by default, a task assigned to a role that writes gets its own git worktree and branch on its first run; read only roles work in the repo folder. Only one run writes in a folder at a time, and QA never reads a tree a Dev is still editing.
- **Integrate into the base**: a task ready to land shows **Integrate...** with a preview: commits ahead and behind, the files that change, and how it reaches the base or why it cannot. The merge commit is built in a temporary copy, never in your checkout, and lands only as a fast-forward of your checkout (on the base branch and clean) or by moving the base branch when it is not checked out anywhere. Nothing is pushed. The verify command of the definition runs only after you **Confirm this command** in its exact text (until then the step is skipped and the dialog says so), and if it fails or times out nothing reaches the base. After any failure your branch, index and files are exactly as they were. **Ask the Dev to rebase** or **Ask the Dev to fix it** sends the task back, and once it is done you can delete its worktree. Repo hooks do not run in this merge, and the dialog says so.
- **Routines and the daily summary**: routines in the definition wake an agent or create a task on a cron schedule. A team created in the app comes with a **Daily summary from the Lead** (on by default, Monday to Friday at 18:00) that lands in **Team notes**. A routine missed while the app was closed runs once at start if less than 2 hours went by; otherwise it is skipped and the Agents tab says so. **Run now** fires one at once.
- **Run silence**: a run that gives no sign of life for a while shows "No signal for 12 min", like a console, with the same minutes from **Settings > Models**. When it looks stuck, a card in **Needs you** offers **Stop the run** or **Keep waiting**.
- **Autonomy and To dispatch**: **Auto dispatch** is per team and off by default. Off, every run the team wants waits in **To dispatch**, with why it was asked and what would hold it, until you **Dispatch** or **Discard** it (or **Dispatch all**). On, runs start by themselves within the team's caps (up to 3 at a time per team and 3 across all teams) and wait while the provider is at its limit.
- **Take control**: **Take control** of any agent opens its conversation in a real console of yours (a running agent asks first, and its run stops). The team does not use that agent until you press **Return to the Team** in the console header or its menu: the console closes and the agent's next run continues that same conversation.
- **On the phone**: a Teams card on Home, the board one column at a time, tasks with their comments and run logs, **Needs you** (approve or ask for changes on a plan or a final OK, approve or reject a permission, answer questions, dispatch runs), new tasks, the chat with the Lead, and push notifications that open the team or the task. It needs the desktop on 2.7.0 with Teams on.
- **What stays on the PC**: hiring a role, **Integrate**, resuming a paused team and reviewing the definition are done on the PC only. The phone shows them read only, marked **On the PC**.
- **Security**: agents of a team never get your console tokens. Each run gets its own key, which only reaches the team's own tools and the usage reading, so with it no agent can type into, open or close your consoles. Read only roles cannot edit files. A permission request shows the exact rule it would grant next to the command that was denied: common test, build, lint and read-only git commands are granted as a prefix, anything else only as that exact command. The app's own keys never make it into a team's board or comments.

### Consoles and desktop

- **What the agent is doing now**: while a tool is open, the pane header, the sidebar row, the agents board and the palette say what it is doing ("Editing a.ts", "Running npm test"), and it goes away when the tool ends.
- **Why a turn failed**: a red chip says why ("Usage limit", "Service overloaded"...) and, while it applies, until when ("until 15:30").
- **Silence**: an agent working with no sign of life shows "No signal for 12 min" and later "Maybe stuck: 25 min without signal". **Ignore this turn** hides it for the rest of the turn; the switch and the minutes (10 and 20 by default) are in **Settings > Models**. Nothing here stops the agent or types into it.
- **Agents can ask you for something**: `snaos needs "<what>"` shows the request as an amber chip without blocking the console, and puts that console first in every "waiting for you" list until it is cleared or you send the next prompt.
- **Context and usage**: a context chip shows how full the conversation is (estimated for Claude, exact for Codex), and its tooltip what the console spent in tokens and cost; the workspace header adds up its consoles. Usage and cost are always marked as estimated.
- **Mark as read and mute**: the pane menu has **Mark as read**, **Mute until it changes** (no badge and no notification until its state changes) and, while it is quiet, **Ignore this turn**. The workspace menu has **Mark all read**.
- **One "waiting for you" rule**: the sidebar badges, **Waiting for you** in the palette, `Ctrl+Shift+J` and the phone count the same consoles in the same order.
- **Read full plan**: a plan waiting for your review opens whole in a read-only window, from the pane header, its menu or the in-app card.
- **Console chat (Labs)**: turn on **Console chat** in **Settings > Labs** for a Terminal | Chat switch on agent consoles. Chat shows the conversation as messages with the tools folded by kind, and the terminal keeps running behind it. Its composer's **Insert** types the text without Enter; only **Send** adds the Enter. A chat paused so as not to open too many at once keeps its messages and waits for **Resume**. A shortcut to switch can be set in **Settings > Keyboard** (none by default).
- **Ctrl+click on `file:line:col`**: in the terminal, Ctrl+click (Cmd+click on macOS) on a path that exists opens it in Files at that line and column, in the console's worktree when it has one.
- **Worktrees**: a "main -N" chip on the branch says how many commits of the main checkout the worktree branch is missing, **New console in this worktree** opens another console in the same folder, and closing a console no longer offers to delete a worktree that other consoles still use.
- **Several schedules per console**: up to 8, each with its own rule, switch, history and **Run now**. **Advanced (cron)** takes a cron expression and shows its next 3 runs; a rule has to leave at least 15 minutes between runs.
- **Calmer notifications**: Web Push, Discord and ntfy send the first 3 notices of a 10 second burst at once and fold the rest into one summary. A turn-complete notice adds which tools ran ("Tools: Edit 3, Read 2"), never the agent's text. Keys and tokens are hidden from notices and from `snaos notify`, and the status sound plays at most once every 2 seconds.
- **Palette search**: results rank exact, prefix, substring and then fuzzy matches, ignoring case and accents.
- **CLI**: `snaos ls`, `snaos peek` and `snaos status` include the failure with its reset time, the silence and the open request; `peek` also shows the turn summary.

### Phone

- **Waiting for you**: a section at the top of Home across all workspaces. The page title, the workspace badges and the waiting pill count the same consoles as the desktop.
- **Richer console rows**: what the agent is doing, the failure and its reset time, the silence, the `snaos needs` request, the mute bell and the context.
- **Mark and mute**: **Mark as read**, **Mute until it changes** and **Ignore this turn** in a console's actions, and **Mark all read** in a workspace's menu.
- **View full plan**: the plan card opens the whole plan in a sheet instead of pointing to the terminal.
- **Several schedules**: Automations shows one card per schedule, cron rules included.
- **Paused chats**: when the PC pauses a chat so as not to open too many at once, the phone says so and waits for you to tap **Resume**.
- These need the desktop on 2.7.0; with an older one they do not show.

### Known limits

- Commands you allowed in `permissions.allow` of your Claude settings also run without asking in the team's Claude runs, read only and plan runs included; only the git operations every team run blocks (push, merge, worktree and update-ref) stay blocked. The setup and **Review definition** say so.
- A role with full access runs any command without asking (a Codex one without a sandbox) and can reach files outside the repo, the app's own data folder included. The setup warns about it: give full access only to the roles that need it.
- Teams use the same Claude and Codex accounts as the app. Automatic account switching never switches in the middle of a team run.
