## What's new in 2.11.0

Hands-free voice: drive your consoles by voice while you game or ride, and hear which ones finished, one voice at a time.

### Hands-free voice (Labs)

Turn it on in **Settings > Labs**. Its settings live in the new **Settings > Voice**. It is Windows x64 only and listens through the local Whisper dictation.

- **Wake word**: hands-free listens all the time, but a phrase counts only when it starts with the wake word.
  - The default is "Agente" in Spanish and "Agent" in English, and you can change it.
  - What you say on Discord or what the game plays is ignored.
  - Say the wake word alone and the next phrase is the command.
- **Voice commands** in Spanish and English:
  - "Agente, workspace MineBosh", "Agente, consola dos", or just a console's name.
  - "Agente, dictale a pagos" starts dictating; "enviar" sends it, "insertar" types it without Enter, "cancelar" drops it.
  - "leé API" and "siguiente" read a reply, "repetí" says the last thing again, "callate" stops the voice, "¿cómo van?" gives the state.
  - "ayuda" lists the commands and "salir" turns hands-free off.
  - Two consoles with close names make it ask which one you mean.
- **Dictation never reaches a console by itself**: what you dictate collects in a panel at the bottom. It goes to the console only when you say "enviar" (with Enter) or "insertar" (without). A console waiting on an answer or showing a menu refuses the send out loud and keeps your text.
- **One voice, one queue**: when consoles finish, ask for permission or fail, it announces them together ("Terminaron API y SnLib"), never two at a time, and you pick which one to hear. Short replies are read as they are. Long ones are summarized in a sentence or two by a quick Haiku run on your own Claude login, ready by the time you ask.
- **Local voice**: Supertonic 3 runs on the CPU, offline, with a male and a female voice. It is a one-time download in Settings > Voice and nothing is bundled.
- **ElevenLabs (optional)**: your own ElevenLabs voice.
  - Fixed phrases ("Terminó API", "Enviado a SnLib") are generated once and kept, so they cost nothing after the first time.
  - By default summaries keep the local voice, so there is no ongoing spend. Switch to **Everything** to use ElevenLabs for summaries too, up to a monthly cap.
  - The API key is stored encrypted on the PC and never shown again.
- **While gaming**: an optional global hotkey turns hands-free on or off while a game has the focus, and hands-free turns itself off after a while without commands. The sidebar shows whether it is listening, dictating or speaking.
- If the narrator mod is on in your Claude settings, Settings > Voice tells you to run `/narrator off`, so two voices never overlap.
