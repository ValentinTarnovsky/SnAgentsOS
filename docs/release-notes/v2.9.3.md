## What's new in 2.9.3

A fix for reordering workspaces in the sidebar.

### Sidebar

- **Dragging a workspace moves it again**: in manual order, dragging a workspace row showed the line where it would land, but dropping it left the order as it was. The drop now moves the row to that place, among favorites or among the rest as before. Dragging a console by its header onto another workspace was not affected.
